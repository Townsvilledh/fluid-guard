    Scan my wallet
    
            
    
              Paste your own wallet address and Guard checks the token
              approvals that wallet has given out — the standing permissions
              that let apps move your tokens. An unlimited approval to a
              contract you do not recognise is how wallets get drained weeks
              after the day they used a dodgy site.
            
    
            
    
              Address only — no wallet connection, no signing, no
              seed phrase, ever. Any “scanner” that asks you to
              connect a wallet or sign a message just to check your
              approvals is using the drainer's own pattern. This scan reads
              public on-chain data — the same data a block explorer shows
              anyone.
            
    
            
    
              
              
              
              
              
    
                
              
    
            
    
            
    
            
            
    
              The scan reads the chain directly from this device through
              public nodes and public block-explorer indexes, and checks
              each approval with Guard's built-in rules — no AI model is
              involved. Depending on the chain it can take a little while;
              progress and any gaps are shown honestly as it goes.

    Why hybrid?
    
            
    
              Every other AI scam checker sends the sensitive data to a server
              to be checked. Guard inverts that. The first pass — the one that
              sees your raw addresses, approvals and messages — runs with the
              Firebase AI Logic mode ONLY_ON_DEVICE, which throws
              rather than falling back: the sensitive pass
              cannot silently leave your device. It works offline and
              costs nothing to run. The cloud is reserved for work that
              genuinely needs a bigger model, and only ever sees a redacted
              summary you approved.
