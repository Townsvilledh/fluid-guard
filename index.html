    Why hybrid?
    
            
    
              Every other AI scam checker sends the sensitive data to a server
              to be checked. Guard inverts that. The first pass — the one that
              sees your raw addresses, approvals and messages — runs with the
              Firebase AI Logic mode ONLY_ON_DEVICE, which throws
              rather than falling back: the sensitive pass
              cannot silently leave your device. It works offline and
              costs nothing to run. The cloud is reserved for work that
              genuinely needs a bigger model, and only ever sees a redacted
              summary you approved.
